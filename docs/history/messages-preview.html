Not shown

  • The coral Atrasada chip, Janela encerrada, and the pink row or card wash could not be shown. The seeded overdue jobs are not in the Ativos window either.
  • The gold Sem faxineiro chip is not on screen. Não atribuídos on Ativos matches nothing, and the overdue jobs that are unassigned cannot load.
